# Orthodontic Design Performance Analytics — Synthetic Case Study

## Problem

Engineering teams need a repeatable way to turn scattered measurement records into a clear view of product-performance variation, design alignment, and investigation priorities.

## Method

A synthetic Python-style workflow:

1. Ingest measurement forms from multiple sources.
2. Preserve arch, tooth-pair, configuration, and reference-distance context.
3. Normalize records for reliable comparisons.
4. Compare baseline and design conditions against reference targets.
5. Aggregate variation by segment and present an engineering review agenda.

## Illustrative findings

- Most synthetic segments align within a defined reference band.
- An anterior transition segment shows the largest target-distance variation.
- A posterior region shows configuration-dependent spread.
- The dashboard converts those observations into named review questions for Design, Quality, and Manufacturing.

## Engineering value

The workflow shortens the path from raw records to a focused root-cause discussion. It supports evidence-based product and process improvement while preserving the need for accountable technical review.

## Public-demo boundary

All records, configurations, metrics, plots, and findings are synthetic. No real patient, employer, product, manufacturing, clinical, or proprietary data is included.
