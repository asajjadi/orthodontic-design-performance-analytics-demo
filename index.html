HOW TO READ THIS

Comparison, not clinical prediction.

The explorer organizes synthetic records by arch, segment, configuration, and target-distance relationship. It helps engineering teams focus review on the variation that warrants follow-up.

ENGINEERING WORKFLOW

  3. Compare against reference targets
  4. Prioritize evidence-based review
